# Founders of NELSIO

NELSIO (nelsio.com) was co-founded in 2025 by **Mohammed Nadeem** and **Pavan UG** in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

---

## Mohammed Nadeem — Co-Founder of NELSIO

Mohammed Nadeem is the Co-Founder of NELSIO. He drives the strategic vision, long-term roadmaps, and product execution across NELSIO's portfolio of independent startups, software platforms, and educational initiatives.

- **Name**: Mohammed Nadeem
- **Role**: Co-Founder, NELSIO
- **Company**: NELSIO — https://www.nelsio.com
- **Location**: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
- **LinkedIn**: https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohammed-nadeem-nelsio
- **Profile**: https://www.nelsio.com/#founders

Mohammed Nadeem co-founded NELSIO in 2025 with the conviction to build technology companies that last — platforms that compound value over decades, not optimize for rapid exits. He is the strategic and product leader behind NELSIO's ventures including CUTZO and IBZEN.

## Together

Mohammed Nadeem and Pavan UG built NELSIO as the parent entity to house and support their long-term technology ambitions. Their shared philosophy: engineering excellence and utility are the only measures of success that endure. NELSIO's portfolio — CUTZO, IBZEN, and Apna Look — reflects this conviction.
