## What NELSIO Does

NELSIO is the unified parent company and developer identity housing distinct platforms, applications, and educational initiatives:

1. **CUTZO** — Flagship Startup: Smart appointment booking and queue management for salons (sector: Commerce & Services)
2. **IBZEN** — Youth Initiative: Immersive career education workshops for school students (sector: Youth Innovation & Career Education)
3. **Apna Look** — Startup Concept: AI-powered outfit recommendations (sector: AI & Fashion-Tech)
